# Fix causes and challenge failed premises

Reproduce the relevant failure, distinguish hypotheses, and remove its mechanism. Ask whether the defect comes from the domain model, ownership, lifecycle, or abstraction itself before adding another special case. Refactor or redesign the necessary part of the system when that is the coherent root-cause solution. Repeated patches that assume the same premise are a reason to revisit it, not to accumulate more guards.

## Apply when

diagnose, deliver, optimize
