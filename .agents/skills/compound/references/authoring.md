# Authoring

Author the trigger for discovery and the body for execution. The body states the outcome, procedure, local rules, and completion evidence without repeating shared guidance. Inline content needed on almost every run; disclose substantial conditional material behind a clear pointer. A reference is not a new discoverable skill. Put role-wide behavior in the agent definition, operational sequencing in its playbook, and a hard requirement at the narrowest applicable rule location. Remove incident names, private IDs, obsolete commands, stock exhortations, and redundant restatements. Validate referenced paths and invocation compatibility. Try realistic positive and negative triggers, including a task that should not call the skill. Keep a manual-only command out of automatic dependency chains. Do not confuse clean prose with demonstrated better agent behavior. The only manual-only packages in this revision are setup and rewrite. compound itself is model-invokable; preserve the distinction between automatic activation and permission to persist an inferred change.
